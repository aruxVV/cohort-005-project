# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around buying a course, having access to it, and getting money back.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who buys or is given a course in order to watch it.
_Avoid_: Student, User

**Purchaser**:
Whoever made a Purchase. For a Team Purchase, this may be someone who never watches the course.
_Avoid_: Buyer, Customer

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
Someone paying for a course, either for themselves or as a Team Purchase. Refund Requests are raised against a Purchase. Buying a course again after a Refund makes a new Purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase of several Seats in one course, bought for a team.

**Seat**:
One place in a Team Purchase. A team member claims it to get an Enrollment. Once the Team Purchase's Refund Request is approved, its unclaimed Seats can no longer be claimed.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence, Code

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a team member has already used to get an Enrollment.

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course, from their own Purchase or from a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Access, Subscription, Registration

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Purchaser's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid. Team members who only claimed a Seat can't raise one. The team approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once. Asking to "cancel a Purchase" is raising a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund. It ends any Enrollment that came from that Purchase and voids any unclaimed Seats.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the Purchaser may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a Purchaser after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. A Team Purchase is refunded whole or not at all, and only if none of its Seats are claimed when the team decides.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute someone raises with their bank or card issuer, not with Cadence. It is not a Refund Request, and the team doesn't review it.
